Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

PlayHuud (the "app") is developed and operated by Eric Baridule Barima ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information PlayHuud collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. It covers the PlayHuud app on iOS and Android and the services it talks to at playhuud.com.

Account information

To play, you create a PlayHuud account one of these ways:

  • Google or Apple sign-in — we receive the basic profile info (name and email address) that Google or Apple shares when you choose to sign in this way.
  • Phone or email verification — if you verify a phone number or email address, we store it against your account so you can recover access to it.
  • Guest play — you can also play without creating an account. We identify a guest session by a random identifier generated on your device, with no phone number or email attached, until and unless you choose to verify one.

Beyond that, we store the display name, username, and avatar you set, and a public encryption key your device generates for secure messaging (explained below) — never a private key, which stays on your device.

Finding friends from your contacts

If you choose to use "Invite from contacts," PlayHuud asks your device for permission to read your contacts. This is entirely optional and only requested when you tap that feature.

When you use it, we send the phone numbers from your contact list (never names) to our server over an encrypted connection to check which numbers match PlayHuud accounts. This includes numbers that do not match an account. We do not retain the submitted number list after processing the request. Your friend relationships are stored so you can connect and play together.

Profiles, photos and gameplay

We store the profile photo you upload, posts and other content you share, game participation, results, ratings, achievements and virtual coin history. If you choose a country, region or city for your competitive profile, we store that approximate location to provide rankings and the profile information you choose to display. We do not request precise device location for these features.

Public posts and live Huud previews can be visible to other users. Publishing a public game invitation makes its preview discoverable even when the Huud is private; the host must approve a request before that visitor joins the game.

Messages and calls

Direct messages between two people are end-to-end encrypted once both of you have exchanged device keys inside the app — meaning the content is unreadable to our servers, which only ever see and store an encrypted blob. Group chat messages, and direct messages sent before that key exchange has happened, are not end-to-end encrypted and are stored on our servers in the same way most messaging apps handle messages, protected by encryption in transit (TLS) and standard access controls.

One-to-one voice calls get an additional layer of end-to-end encryption on top of standard call security once a key exchange has happened; group calls are protected by standard transport encryption (DTLS-SRTP) only. All calls run on voice infrastructure we operate ourselves — we don't send your call data to a third-party calling vendor.

Microphone use in Word Bluff

Word Bluff's optional voice-detection feature uses the operating system's speech-recognition service. Depending on your device and the service's settings and capabilities, recognition may use on-device processing or the operating system provider's service. PlayHuud does not send the recognition audio or transcript to its own servers; it sends a correct-guess gameplay signal. You can disable voice matching in PlayHuud settings and change speech-recognition permission in your device settings.

Coins and the in-game wallet

PlayHuud games use an in-game virtual currency ("coins") that you earn by playing matches. Coins cannot be purchased with real money, and cannot be withdrawn, exchanged, cashed out, or redeemed for real money or anything of monetary value — they exist only inside the app, for gameplay. Some game rooms let players optionally wager coins against each other, with the winner taking the pot; this never involves real money in either direction. We keep a ledger of your coin balance and history tied to your account.

Device and diagnostic information

We use account and device identifiers to operate sign-in and notifications. Our own services record sign-in events, gameplay activity and operational errors to understand usage and keep the service working. These records may be linked to your account. We do not integrate third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

Service providers support infrastructure, uploaded-file delivery, email-code delivery and push notifications. These include our hosting and storage providers, Brevo for email delivery when configured, and Firebase Cloud Messaging with Apple's notification service for iOS push notifications. Google or Apple handles sign-in when you choose its sign-in option.

How we use your information

  • To create and run your account, and to let you play with and message other players.
  • To match you with friends already using PlayHuud, when you choose to use that feature.
  • To operate voice calls and real-time gameplay.
  • To keep the service secure and working as intended.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own advertising or marketing purposes.

Your choices

  • Contacts, microphone, and other device permissions are optional and can be changed any time in your device's app settings.
  • You can permanently delete your account, including a guest account, in PlayHuud under Settings → Delete account. You must confirm the deletion. Apple-linked accounts also require confirmation through Apple so we can revoke their Apple authorization.
  • Deletion removes your profile, contact details, friend relationships, messages and posts you authored, personal game records and virtual-coin balance. Groups and Huuds you host are removed and affected games end. Other players retain their own accounts and rating records without your player identity. Historical ownership may be displayed as “Deleted player”.
  • Stored outfit images are queued for removal, with retries if storage is temporarily unavailable. Restricted infrastructure backups may retain earlier copies pending backup rotation; they are not used to restore individual deleted accounts. Copies already held by other people or their devices cannot be recalled.
  • You can also request account deletion without the app, or contact us about access and correction using the email address below. Signing in after deletion creates a new account; it does not recover the deleted account.

Age suitability

PlayHuud is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. A store or region may apply a higher minimum age. This audience setting is not a claim that PlayHuud verifies every user's age. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and address the request.
