Delete your PlayHuud account

You can delete your account and associated personal data in PlayHuud or request deletion without opening the app.

Delete in the app

  1. Open PlayHuud and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings → Delete account.
  3. Review what will be removed and confirm. Apple-linked accounts also require confirmation through Apple.

Guest accounts can also be deleted in Settings. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

Request deletion without the app

Email kingbarric@yahoo.com with the subject PlayHuud account deletion request. Include your PlayHuud username and the email address linked to the account. Where possible, send the request from that address. We verify ownership before processing a request and confirm the outcome by email.

Never send a password, verification code, private key or payment information. If you cannot access your linked email, explain this in your request so we can help verify ownership.

Request account deletion

What is removed

Deletion removes your profile, contact details, friend relationships, messages and posts you authored, personal game records and virtual-coin balance. Groups and Huuds you host are removed and affected games end. Other players keep their own accounts and rating records without your player identity; historical ownership may appear as “Deleted player”.

Retention and limitations

Stored outfit images are queued for removal, with retries if storage is temporarily unavailable. Restricted infrastructure backups may retain earlier copies pending backup rotation and are not used to restore individual deleted accounts. Copies already held by other people or their devices cannot be recalled. Deletion is permanent; signing in again creates a new account.
