Traitors

Social deduction

One table, hidden roles, and one person who isn't who they say they are. Every game secretly seeds a handful of Traitors among the Faithful — nobody knows who until the votes start landing. Talk your way through the Round Table, read the room, and vote out who you think is lying before the Traitors thin your numbers overnight. Five modes ship at launch, from Classic Conspiracy — pure deduction, no powers, roles revealed on elimination — up to Blood Moon, where a second night kill unlocks once the Traitors get desperate.

Word Bluff

Spin & describe

Two teams, one spinning wheel, and a clock that never feels long enough. Spin lands you on a category, flip the card, and describe the word on it any way you can — except the word itself — while your team races to shout the answer before time runs out. Get it right and the points roll in; get stuck and you can skip once and move on to the next. First team to the target score takes the win. It's loud, it's fast, and somebody's always convinced their clue was obvious.

Draughts

1v1 · flying kings

Draughts, played properly. A full 10×10 international board, flying kings that can cross the whole board in a single move, and forced captures — if you can take a piece, you have to. There's no casual shuffling here: every open lane is a decision, and missing a capture costs you the piece you should have taken instead. It's strictly one-on-one, there's no draw to hide behind, and a replay button is always there for the move that decided the whole game.

1v1only
10×10board

Whot

2–20 players

The card game that's been round every family table for a generation, brought online. Match the card in play by shape or by number, stack specials to make the next player pick up or skip their go, and hold onto a Whot card for the moment you need to call whatever shape saves you. Anywhere from two to twenty players can sit at one table at once — shed your entire hand before anyone else, and the round is yours.
